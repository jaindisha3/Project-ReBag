01 — About Us

Less polythene.
More possibility.

Project Re-Bag is designed around one simple thought: people do not always need another bag — they need an easy way to reuse the bags that already exist.

01 / ACCESS

Easy to borrow

Re-Bag stations can be placed where people actually shop — from local markets and societies to everyday community spaces.

02 / REUSE

Made to return

Instead of taking another single-use bag, users borrow a reusable one and return it after use.

03 / COMMUNITY

Built together

The model encourages communities to share responsibility for a cleaner, more reusable everyday environment.

02 — What We Do

A bag station
that keeps moving.

A Re-Bag station acts as a shared library for reusable bags. A user can borrow a bag when needed, use it for their purchase, and return it to a nearby station.

The system can use simple QR-based tracking, helper logins, reminders and rewards to encourage returns. The bags themselves can be made from materials such as upcycled fabric, jute and paper.

The goal isn't to make people buy another “eco-friendly product.” It's to make reuse part of the normal routine.
